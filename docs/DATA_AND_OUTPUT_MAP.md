## Raw inputs required for the extended validation and sensitivity analyses

- PVGIS hourly Stuttgart CSV, 2005–2023.
- Raw gridded NEX-GDDP-CMIP6 `tasmax` files for the 5×5 Stuttgart subset, required for the spatial sensitivity.
- Baseline `THERMAL_DAILY_STUTTGART.csv` generated by stages 04–06.

## Extended validation and sensitivity outputs

Stage 11 produces hourly Arrhenius and energy-index validation metrics, monthly correction factors, calibrated daily stress/energy indices, and propagated base-case period results.

Stage 12 produces model-specific and ensemble heatwave/non-heatwave stress decomposition plus a counterfactual service-life calculation.

Stage 13 produces grid metadata, model/method and ensemble spatial heatwave summaries, and future/historical ratios for grid-cell, bilinear and regional treatments.

Stage 14 produces the factorial structural uncertainty table and scenario envelopes.

Compact successful-run copies are retained in `reference_results/extended_validation/`.
